How it's counted

  • Types and the roles ramp, draw, removal, wipes and tutors come from this app's own classifier (classify.js), run on both decks. Each card counts as one main type.
  • I took The Mind Stone out of removal by hand, because the classifier misreads it. Both decks still count Boros Charm and Mjölnir as removal, and Mjölnir and Ori as wipes.
  • Protection, Equipment, Dwarves and Treasure makers are simple text and type checks.
  • Average deck: EDHREC Dáin, bracket 3 (Upgraded), fetched 18 Nov 2026.
