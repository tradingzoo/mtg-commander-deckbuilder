The short version

  1. Artifacts over creatures is the right call. Red is the most fragile colour, and Equipment survives wipes and removal that kill Dwarves. That matches what your pod does. Keep creatures around 28-30, and let artifacts go up.
  2. Make the generic slots wear the theme. Your ramp is already artifacts. Do the same with removal (Slash of Light, Judgment Bolt) and draw (Swordsman's Steel, Minas Tirith).
  3. Skip the third card. Every Equipment needs equip mana. One mover (Forge Anew, Brass Squire, Ardenn, Halvar) pays for all of them. You already run Puresteel, Kíli, Sram, Reyav and Steelshaper's Gift, so this is about adding 1-2 more.
  4. Curve to Dáin on turn 3. 1-mana rock on turn 1, a 3-mana play on turn 2, Dáin on turn 3. Your 2-mana rocks are the weakest ramp slot.
  5. Have 3-4 wins that aren't Dáin. Equipment that scales (Swordsman's Steel, Colossus Hammer with free equips, Embercleave), and artifact pings (Reckless Fireweaver with your Treasure makers).
  6. Your list is 101 cards (28 creatures, 22 artifacts with 13 Equipment, 35 lands). Every add below needs a cut. Pair them up out → in.
